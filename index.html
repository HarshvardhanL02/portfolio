twinsparktech.online

TwinSparkTech Platform

Parent platform

A complete MHT-CET platform that helps students predict colleges, analyze cutoffs, explore seat matrices, estimate ranks, and generate recommendations — with counselling tools for the mentors guiding them.

ProblemMHT-CET admissions data is scattered, hard to search, and mostly locked inside government PDFs — students and counsellors were left triangulating decisions manually.
SolutionA Next.js platform on Cloudflare Workers that unifies prediction, cutoff exploration, seat matrix data and counselling workflows on one shared data layer.
ImpactThe parent platform tying every system below into one connected admissions workflow.
Frontend Next.js Backend Cloudflare Workers Database Firebase Infra Cloudflare

Infrastructure
  • extract_pipeline.py

    Government PDF Data Engine

    The pipeline that turns raw, unstructured government counselling PDFs into the structured JSON every TwinSparkTech tool runs on.

    ProblemGovernment CAP and counselling data is published as unstructured, inconsistent PDFs — unusable directly by any prediction or search system.
    SolutionBuilt a JavaScript/Python extraction pipeline, open-sourced as CET Extractor, then upgraded it into a dedicated engine that parses, validates and normalizes records into production-ready JSON.
    ImpactPowers the data layer behind the College Predictor and every other tool in the ecosystem.
    JavaScript Python PDF Processing Data Validation
    View code ↗
  • counseldesk (private)

    CounselDesk CRM

    A counselling management platform built for mentors and education consultants — the operational side of MHT-CET admissions, separate from the student-facing tools.

    ProblemMentors and consultants running MHT-CET counselling had no dedicated system — just spreadsheets and manual tracking for students, documents and bookings.
    SolutionA full CRM on Cloudflare D1 and R2 with role-based dashboards covering the counselling workflow end-to-end, from lead to booking to recommendation.
    ImpactGives mentors one system for the full counselling workflow instead of scattered spreadsheets.
    Student Management Lead Tracking Booking Management Payment Tracking Role-Based Access

About

Who I am

I'm a first-year Mechanical Engineering student at MITAOE, Pune, who spends most of my time building software. What started as a single college-admission predictor grew into TwinSparkTech — an ecosystem of prediction tools, a government data pipeline, and a counselling CRM used across the MHT-CET admissions cycle.

I care about shipping things that work over things that look impressive in a submission — extracting real data out of messy government PDFs, caching it properly, and building interfaces that get out of the way.

Currently focused on: making TwinSparkTech faster and more reliable, and going deeper on backend and cloud fundamentals.
